01

Principles and voice

Calm, precise, plain. Never selling, never alarming. Trust is the product: every number and route statement has its source, evidence level and date one tap away.

Always

  • These are the household's own papers, never issued by an authority. Stamps are "evidence marks".
  • Route results say "Appears covered", "Appears partly covered", "Not covered yet", and "conditions that appear to apply". Never "eligible", never a bare "Covered".
  • Every route result keeps the note "This is not a visa or residence decision. Only the authority decides." and the disclaimer line.
  • Before the household exists, answers come from the criteria ("Meets all your Non-negotiables", "Climate not known yet"). The route shows only as the route check label.
  • Unknown is a calm state with its reason, never an empty cell, a zero or a hidden row. Not yet and unknown share one mark: a light solid line, never dashes or dots.
  • The evidence band is capped at Partial while one of the household's Non-negotiables is unknown, with the one line that says why.
  • The disclaimer is the same everywhere: every footer, phone included, carries the privacy promise and the two links, Disclaimer and Terms of use. Nothing in the body.
  • Oxblood is for real next steps only; reading pages have a Back link instead.
  • Citrus highlights at most one value per card, never a stale, unknown or flagged value.
  • Text never sits on a photo. Every photo has a credit line: "Drone photo: [credit]" or "Photo: [name] / Unsplash".

Never

  • A winner, crown, medal, rank number, "best match" or confidence percentage.
  • A paywall, price, upsell, newsletter, ad, referral link or anything that sells.
  • Flags. The country code and the postage-stamp photo identify a country.
  • Tape, corners or clips on photos. The large watermark or the lighter pattern options.
  • Meaning carried by color alone, hover-only interaction, text below 0.75rem, fractional pixels.
  • Anything loaded from another website: fonts, icons, map outlines and photos are served from our own site.
  • Anything about the household in a web address.
02

Color, light and dark

Every color is a named token. Light is the base for screens. Dark is designed, not inverted: night-navy paper, raised dark cards, lighter inks that still read as ink, photo prints with a dark border, and the A2 pattern retuned to keep its quiet presence. Values are read live from the stylesheet.

Label rules

  • Uppercase only for short labels of one to three words: NATIONALITIES, AGE, LAST CHECKED.
  • Anything longer, such as the state line under a component name, is set in sentence case at the same size.
  • A label and its value sit on the same text line and share a first baseline. When the value wraps, the label stays with its first line.

Touch and focus

  • Every control and every small mark (note marks, evidence marks, coverage marks, rail legs) has a hit area of at least 44 × 44px, even where the mark is smaller.
  • Visible 2px focus ring in stamp violet. Full keyboard use: criteria move with keys 1 to 4.
  • Nothing needs hover. Tooltips open on tap or focus.

Not yet, unknown: the light mark

One mark for everything that is not there yet, system-wide: a light solid line in the hairline ink, never dashes or dots. Tokens --ink-notyet, --b-notyet (2px) and --b-notyet-thin (1px). Used by the E0 evidence mark, unknown values, the not-yet step circle, the "coming" tag, the boarding pass destination underline, yes/no criterion cards, empty piles, drop zones and empty states. The word on the mark carries the meaning; the line stays quiet. The plan rail's connectors are plain lines, solid green after a finished step.

06

Iconography

Iconoir only, regular set, 1.5 stroke in its own drawing units, sized 16, 20 or 24px. Served from our own site.

07

Pattern, seal, wordmark, evidence marks

Background pattern A2: five waves on a 360 × 160 tile in v3's navy-gray and violet-gray, 1px lines, about 1.3:1 on the paper. Used for the page, the app screens and the thin bands on cards. Readable content always sits on the white paper cards.

A2, light and dark

Tokens: --pattern-line-1, --pattern-line-2, --pattern-size. No other pattern options, no large watermark.

Wordmark

The monogram at 32px, then "The Emigrant" in Archivo condensed. The wordmark leads to Start, or to the plan once one exists.

The seal and the small mark

Seal, 64px and up. The outer double ring. From 140px, "THE EMIGRANT" along the top and "EVIDENCE MARK" along the bottom, both upright, with the same clear space to the outer and the inner ring, and a dot between the arcs on each side. Inside, one band of loops runs between two circles, about a third as many loops as before (), with space between them. The center stays open paper and carries the monogram. From 64 to 120px the lettering is dropped.
Monogram, 48px and below. A condensed "E" in a double ring, drawn separately for 16, 24, 32 and 48px. Used for the wordmark, the browser tab icon and the E3 evidence mark. Whole-pixel lines in stamp violet.
